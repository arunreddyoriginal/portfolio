You are an autonomous SEO strategist + technical SEO engineer + content optimization specialist.

Your task is to transform my EXISTING website into a highly search-engine-optimized website while preserving its current visual identity, functionality, and overall design.

The website should become easier for Google and other search engines to understand, crawl, index, categorize, and rank for searches that are genuinely relevant to me.

The objective is NOT to chase broad, highly competitive keywords.

The objective is to establish a clear, specific niche around who I am, what I do, what I build, and what types of users/clients/recruiters should discover me.

Work from the existing website and repository.

Do not rebuild the website from scratch.

==================================================
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Improve the website across:

1. SEO positioning
2. niche definition
3. keyword strategy
4. search intent alignment
5. on-page SEO
6. technical SEO
7. content quality
8. semantic HTML
9. structured data
10. internal linking
11. crawlability/indexability
12. performance
13. accessibility where it affects discoverability
14. image SEO
15. page metadata
16. entity clarity
17. topical relevance

The final result should clearly communicate to search engines:

WHO I AM

WHAT I DO

WHAT I SPECIALIZE IN

WHAT TECHNOLOGIES / SERVICES / SKILLS I AM RELEVANT FOR

WHAT TYPES OF PROJECTS I BUILD

WHO MY WORK IS FOR

and, only if factually relevant:

WHERE I AM BASED / WHAT LOCATION I SERVE.

==================================================
IMPORTANT SEO PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Do NOT optimize for "more keywords."

Optimize for:

RELEVANCE
+
SEARCH INTENT
+
TOPICAL CLARITY
+
CONTENT QUALITY
+
TECHNICAL ACCESSIBILITY
+
ENTITY CLARITY.

Do not use keyword stuffing.

PHASE 3 — DETERMINE MY ACTUAL NICHE
==================================================

This is one of the most important stages.

Do NOT invent a niche based only on what seems good for SEO.

Determine my niche from evidence already present in:

- website
- projects
- About section
- skills
- experience
- technologies
- design style
- services if offered

Look for recurring strengths.

For example, a generic positioning such as:

"Web Developer"

may be too broad.

A stronger niche might look conceptually like:

"Frontend developer specializing in interactive web experiences"

or:

"React developer focused on animated portfolio and product websites"

or:

"Creative frontend developer combining Figma, React and motion design"

BUT ONLY use positioning supported by my actual work.

Do not invent capabilities I do not demonstrate.

==================================================
NICHE SELECTION CRITERIA
==================================================

Find a niche that balances:

SPECIFICITY

It should be specific enough for search engines and visitors to understand.

ACCURACY

It must reflect my real abilities.

SEARCH DEMAND

People should plausibly search for related services/skills.

DIFFERENTIATION

It should distinguish me from millions of generic developer portfolios.

CONTENT SUPPORT

My projects and skills should provide evidence for the positioning.

EXPANSION

The niche should still allow the website to grow later.
